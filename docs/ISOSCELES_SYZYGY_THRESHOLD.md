# Isosceles first-syzygy threshold

## Scope

This note resolves the singular torque-threshold geometry at the
isosceles-right corner and proves a one-sided local theorem for the continued
first syzygy.  It does **not** prove nonperiodicity in a punctured endpoint
neighborhood: the interior torque-contact barrier and the post-encounter
dynamics remain open.

Write

\[
 u=(\sqrt2-1)v,
 \qquad
 q={nz\over m+n},
 \qquad 0<v\leq1,\quad 0<z<1,
\]

on the torque-compatible ordered syzygy face.  Here
\(q=r_{31}/r_{12}\), body 3 is between bodies 1 and 2, and
\((m,n,1)=(A(u),B(u),1)\).

## A uniform amplitude gap

The cancelled torque-contact threshold from `EXACT_REDUCTIONS.md` satisfies

\[
 \boxed{Z_J^{\rm syz}>1}
 \tag{1}
\]

on the complete open tied face.  This strengthens the previous result
\(0<Z_J^{\rm syz}<Z_2\).

Indeed, the denominator of \(Z_J^{\rm syz}-1\) is positive by the already
certified signs of \(q\),
\(D=mn+mq^2+n(1-q)^2\), and \(D_J\).  Pull the numerator back to the
\((v,z)\) square and clear only positive mass and mass-sum denominators.  The
result has bidegree \((27,10)\).  Its 308 exact
\(\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)\) tensor-Bernstein coefficients comprise 285 positive
and 23 zero coefficients.  On the equal-mass edge, the first ten of eleven
coefficients are positive and only the singular \(z=1\) endpoint coefficient
vanishes.  Thus (1) is strict everywhere in the stated domain.

The deterministic generators are
`ordered_syzygy_torque_amplitude_sign_cores` and

## Critical-scale monotonicity

For fixed tied masses, the energy critical scale

\[
 R_J={U-FZ_J^{\rm syz}/2\over U_0}
\]

is strictly decreasing as \(q\) increases through
\(0<q<n/(m+n)\):

\[
 \boxed{\partial_qR_J<0.}
 \tag{2}
\]

The denominator of the derivative is

\[
q^2(1-q)^2(m^2n^2+1)D_J^2>0.
\]

After removing the positive factor \(mn\) and applying the tied-square
substitution, the numerator has bidegree \((30,12)\).  Its 403 exact
Bernstein coefficients are 374 negative and 29 zero.  The equal-mass edge
retains ten negative coefficients for \(0<z<1\).  This proves (2), including
arbitrarily small positive separations.

This monotonicity is a structural simplification, not the missing history
bound: the first-syzygy trajectories observed in the family have
\(R<R_J\), so the longitudinal kinetic term remains essential.

## Blow-up of the singular corner

The point \((v,z)=(1,1)\) is a genuine direction-dependent corner.  Put

\[
 v=1-\varepsilon,
 \qquad z=1-\lambda\varepsilon,
 \qquad \lambda\geq0.
\]

Define

\[
 \kappa={28-3\sqrt2\over11},
\]

and

\[
\begin{aligned}
 Z_0&={21(1+\sqrt2)\over22},&
 Z_\infty&={3836+2709\sqrt2\over6128},\\
 R_0&={11+2\sqrt2\over55},&
 R_\infty&={-361+2273\sqrt2\over3830}.
\end{aligned}
\]

First directional jets of the exact rational functions give

\[
 \boxed{
 \lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}Z_J^{\rm syz}
 ={Z_0+\kappa\lambda Z_\infty\over1+\kappa\lambda}},
 \tag{3}
\]

\[
 \boxed{
 \lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}R_J
 ={R_0+\kappa\lambda R_\infty\over1+\kappa\lambda}}.
 \tag{4}
\]

Here \(Z_0>Z_\infty>1\) and \(R_0<R_\infty\).  Thus the two iterated
limits do not commute.  In particular, substituting equal masses before
taking the torque edge is not the dynamical endpoint limit.  The exact
directional-jet residuals are generated by
`ordered_syzygy_endpoint_corner_blowup` and vanish identically.

## Validated endpoint event and tangent

At the symmetric endpoint write the base positions as
\((-x,y_b),(x,y_b)\), the apex as \((0,y_a)\), and
\(h=y_a-y_b\).  The reduced equations are

\[
 \ddot x=-{1\over4\sqrt2\,x^2}
           -{x\over(x^2+h^2)^{3/2}},
 \qquad
 \ddot h=-{(1+\sqrt2)h\over(x^2+h^2)^{3/2}}.
\]

The first syzygy is \(h=0\).  At that event

\[
 Z={\ell_{23}^2\over r_{12}}={x\dot h^2\over2}.
\]

The pinned native-interval CAPD replay in
`data/isosceles_syzygy_capd.log` encloses every Taylor step before the first
crossing, proves all separations positive, proves a unique downward crossing,
and gives

\[
 \boxed{Z_*<1.}
 \tag{5}
\]

A separate 14-dimensional \(C^1\) Poincare-map calculation treats the masses
as frozen state coordinates and propagates the exact tied launch tangent

\[
 (m_{1,v},m_{2,v},q_{3x,v})=(-1/2,1/2,1/\sqrt2).
\]

For the continued first-syzygy event, let
\(z(v)=q(v)(m(v)+n(v))/n(v)\).  It proves

\[
 \boxed{\lambda_*=z_v(1)>18>0.}
 \tag{6}
\]

The stored hexadecimal enclosure is approximately centered at

the event is transverse.

## One-sided local consequence

Smooth dependence and the implicit-function theorem continue this first
syzygy for \(v<1\) sufficiently close to one.  Equations (5)--(6) give

\[
 z(v)=1-\lambda_*(1-v)+O((1-v)^2)<1,
 \qquad Z(v)<1.
\]

The continued event therefore lies on the torque-compatible ordered face,
and (1) yields

\[
 \boxed{Z(v)<1<Z_J^{\rm syz}(v,z(v))}
 \tag{7}
\]

through an existential one-sided neighborhood of the isosceles endpoint.
This is **PROVED BY COMPUTER-ASSISTED ARGUMENT** together with exact symbolic
inequalities.  It closes the *terminal first-syzygy amplitude obligation* in
that neighborhood.  It does not exclude an earlier interior contact, a later
second brake, or the close base-pair encounter that unfolds the endpoint
