## Shape equation and elliptic coordinates

On either zero-energy heavy-binary half, the planar restricted shape \(z\)
satisfies

\[
 z_{\zeta\zeta}+{1\over3}z_\zeta=\nabla W(z),\qquad
 W(z)={1\over9}\left(
 |z|^2+{1\over r_+}+{1\over r_-}\right),
\tag{1}
\]

where \(r_+=|z-1/2|\) and \(r_-=|z+1/2|\). Introduce complex half-angle
variables

\[
 s=\sin(\xi/2),\qquad c=\cos(\xi/2),\qquad s^2+c^2=1,
\tag{2}
\]

and put

\[
 z={c^2-s^2\over2}={1\over2}\cos\xi,\qquad
 A={dz\over d\xi}=-sc,\qquad g=|A|^2.
\tag{3}
\]

Then

\[
 z-{1\over2}=-s^2,\quad z+{1\over2}=c^2,\quad
 r_+=|s|^2,\quad r_-=|c|^2,\quad g=r_+r_-.
\tag{4}
\]

Thus \(s=0\) and \(c=0\) are the two collision points, while the elliptic
metric factor is exactly the product of their distances. Let

\[
 E={|z_\zeta|^2\over2}-W(z),\qquad
 p=g\xi_\zeta=\overline A z_\zeta,\qquad
 {d\zeta\over d\sigma}=\eta g,\quad \eta\in\{+1,-1\}.
\tag{5}
\]

The physical continuation after the fifth outgoing section has decreasing
heavy-collision time and uses \(\eta=-1\).

## Polynomial regularized field

Define

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 K_0&={2\over9}Az\left(\overline A^2-\overline z^2\right),\\
 K_{\rm pair}&={s\overline c-c\overline s\over9},\\
 \mathcal P&=-2A\overline zE+K_0+K_{\rm pair}-{g\over3}p.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]

Then (1) is equivalent away from the foci to

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 s_\sigma&=\eta {cp\over2},&
 c_\sigma&=-\eta {sp\over2},\\
 p_\sigma&=\eta\mathcal P,&
 E_\sigma&=-\eta {|p|^2\over3},\\
 \zeta_\sigma&=\eta g.
 \end{aligned}}
\tag{7}
\]

In real and imaginary parts, (7) is polynomial and contains no division by a
primary distance. The two singular force terms cancel separately:

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 2A\overline{z_{\xi\xi}}{1\over r_+}
 +\overline A g\left(-{z-1/2\over r_+^3}\right)
 &=-c\overline s,\\
 2A\overline{z_{\xi\xi}}{1\over r_-}
 +\overline A g\left(-{z+1/2\over r_-^3}\right)
 &=s\overline c.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]

The common factor \(1/9\) gives \(K_{\rm pair}\). The quadratic potential
gives \(K_0\), and \(z_{\xi\xi}=-z\) gives the energy term.

For interval propagation it is preferable not to evolve redundant \(s,c\):
a generic doubleton does not preserve \(s^2+c^2=1\) exactly. The
nonredundant coordinate \(\xi=\alpha+i\beta\) reconstructs

\[

   +i\cos(\alpha/2)\sinh(\beta/2),\\
 c&=\cos(\alpha/2)\cosh(\beta/2)
   -i\sin(\alpha/2)\sinh(\beta/2).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{8a}
\]

If \(s=x+iy\) lies on the fifth-section sheet near zero, an analytic inverse

\[

 {\sqrt{(x+1)^2+y^2}-\sqrt{(x-1)^2+y^2}\over2}\right),\\

Here \(\operatorname{arsinh}q=\log(q+\sqrt{1+q^2})\), so every operation is
